What applies to you · California

Two laws. Both hit small teams.

We keep it to what actually reaches a small California AI company — no eight-jurisdiction sprawl, no enterprise theater. Just the two that matter for you right now.

In effect now · no size threshold

AB 2013 — Training-Data Transparency

Any developer that builds, fine-tunes, or substantially modifies a public generative-AI system available to Californians must publish specific training-data disclosures. There is no user-count minimum — it applies to you.

Cal. Civ. Code § 3111 · operative Jan 1, 2026
Deadline · August 2, 2026

SB 942 — AI Transparency Act

Provenance disclosures, an AI-content verification tool, and latent disclosure for generated content. SB 1000 (moving in the Assembly) would delete the current 1M-user carve-out — pulling in the small teams too.

Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 22757 et seq. · verify subsections w/ counsel
